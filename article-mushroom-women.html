Mushroom growing gives Mbale women a new route to income

September 8, 2026 • Mbale, Uganda • By Mbale Media Daily

Women in Mbale are turning mushroom cultivation into a commercial activity, using controlled growing conditions to produce edible mushrooms and create income.

The Mushroom Growers in Mbale group, led by Annet Shakira Mutende, has used the enterprise as an alternative livelihood. The group began mushroom growing in 2011 and members say the activity can help with food, school fees and other household needs.

Mushrooms can be grown on relatively small spaces, making the enterprise attractive to farmers who do not have large areas of land. The women market fresh and dried mushrooms to households and other buyers.

However, growers face challenges including input costs, access to quality spawn, inconsistent prices, storage limitations and the need for reliable markets. Expanding production therefore requires not only farming skills but also stronger marketing and value addition.

The Mbale experience shows how small-scale agricultural enterprises can create additional income while contributing to food security.
