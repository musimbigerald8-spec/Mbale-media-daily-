Uganda beat Kenya by 64 runs in Africa Continental Cup

September 12, 2026 • Kigali, Rwanda • By Mbale Media Daily

Uganda defeated Kenya by 64 runs in the Africa Continental Cup in Kigali on Saturday, September 12, strengthening the Cricket Cranes' campaign in the regional tournament.

The result was reported by live match listings and cricket coverage for the ninth match of the competition at Gahanga Cricket Stadium.

The victory gives Uganda an important result against a regional rival as the team continues its continental campaign.

Match source: International Cricket Council match centre and live cricket listings, September 12, 2026. ICC.
