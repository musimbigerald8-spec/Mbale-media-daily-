Uganda face Kenya in Africa Continental Cup

September 12, 2026 • Sports Desk • By Mbale Media Daily

Uganda face Kenya in Match 9 of the Africa Continental Cup in Kigali on Saturday, September 12, with the Cricket Cranes entering the fixture after a strong start to the tournament.

Uganda opened the competition with a 114-run win over Sierra Leone, followed by a five-wicket victory over Botswana and a 64-run win over Rwanda. The results put Uganda among the teams to watch as the group campaign continues.

Kenya also entered the fixture with wins over Sierra Leone, Rwanda and Botswana, setting up a competitive regional contest at Gahanga Cricket Stadium Oval B.

For Mbale Media Daily readers, the Kenya fixture is an important test of Uganda's consistency after the team's strong opening performances. Results and confirmed match details will be updated as the tournament progresses.
