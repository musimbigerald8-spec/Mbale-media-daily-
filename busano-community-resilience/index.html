WHO WE ARE

A local initiative in development.

Busano Community Resilience Initiative (BCRI) is being established as a locally rooted platform focused on disaster preparedness, risk reduction and community resilience in Busano, Uganda.

Our starting point is evidence: listening to residents, documenting hazards and priority needs, strengthening local coordination and building partnerships before scaling activities.

THE CHALLENGE

Risk requires preparation before the next emergency.

Mt. Elgon communities face recurring landslide and flood risks. BCRI will document local conditions carefully rather than publish unverified impact figures.

Disaster risk

Government and humanitarian sources identify landslides, mudslides, flooding and soil erosion among important hazards in Uganda's highland areas, including the Elgon region.

Local vulnerability

Past reporting has documented landslide displacement in Busano, including villages in the Buwangwa and Shitega/Shiteka area. These historical reports will guide questions for a new, verified baseline.

Preparedness gap

Community preparedness works best when residents, local government, humanitarian actors and technical specialists can coordinate before and during emergencies.

Background sources: Uganda Office of the Prime Minister; Oxfam in Uganda; Uganda Radio Network and other documented reporting. Sources are provided for context, not as BCRI impact claims.

WHAT WE PLAN TO DO

Four connected areas of work

01

Early warning & awareness

Community information channels, local risk mapping and practical preparedness messages during periods of heavy rain and other hazards.

02

Emergency readiness

Develop plans for temporary shelter, basic emergency supplies, safe referrals and coordinated response when incidents occur.

03

Resilient communities

Support appropriate drainage, environmental restoration, preparedness and livelihood-resilience actions with qualified partners.

04

Safer relocation pathways

Where technical assessments identify serious danger, support dialogue and coordination with authorities and partners around lawful, safe and dignified options.
