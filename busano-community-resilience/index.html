WHY THIS EXISTS

Prepared communities can respond faster.

Heavy rains can expose households, roads, water sources and other community assets to landslide and related disaster risks. BCRI is being developed as a locally rooted platform to help communities understand risk, strengthen preparedness and connect with organisations able to provide appropriate support.

We are starting with evidence: listening to residents, documenting risks and working toward a verified community needs assessment before scaling activities.

WHAT WE PLAN TO DO

From preparedness to resilience

01

Early warning

Community information channels, risk mapping and practical preparedness messages for periods of heavy rain.

02

Emergency readiness

Develop plans for temporary shelter, basic emergency supplies, referrals and coordinated response when incidents occur.

03

Resilient communities

Support appropriate environmental, drainage, preparedness and livelihood-resilience actions with qualified partners.

04

Safer relocation

Where assessments show households are in serious danger, work with authorities and partners on lawful, voluntary and safe relocation pathways.

OUR APPROACH

Evidence. Coordination. Accountability.

01Listen & assess — document affected locations, households, infrastructure and priority needs.
02Prepare — strengthen community awareness, local coordination and response planning.
03Partner — connect community priorities with district authorities, NGOs, humanitarian agencies and funders.
04Report — publish clear updates on funding, activities, results and lessons.
