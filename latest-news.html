BREAKING • SECURITY • KAMPALA

Fire destroys property at Wandegeya Police barracks

A fire broke out at Wandegeya Police barracks at about 3pm on Thursday, October 15. More than 10 fire brigade vehicles and other emergency teams responded. Police said no injuries had been reported and the cause was still under investigation.
