EASTERN UGANDA • BUDUDA

Bududa leaders arrested over alleged PDM funds diversion

Three local leaders were arrested after an allegation that Shs1 million in Parish Development Model funds belonging to a beneficiary was accessed using her national ID and sent to a phone number registered in one suspect's name.

The case was reported on September 16 as authorities investigated the alleged diversion. The allegation remains subject to investigation and due process.

Mbale Media Daily will follow developments and report verified information as it becomes available.

EASTERN UGANDA • BULAMBULI

Bulambuli launches Shs200m rehabilitation of Bunambutye-Girigi Road

Bulambuli District leaders have launched a Shs200 million rehabilitation project for the Bunambutye-Girigi road, with the work intended to improve movement along the route.

The road has remained difficult to access because of its condition. District officials said rehabilitation should make transport and access easier for communities that use the route.

KUMI • SECURITY

Eight youths held over night attacks in Kumi Municipality

Police and local leaders say eight youths have been arrested during a joint security operation targeting suspected night attacks in South Division, Kumi Municipality.

Local authorities said night attacks have become a growing concern, with residents seeking stronger protection during the early hours. The arrests are part of an ongoing investigation.

BUSINESS • UGANDA

NSSF revenue rises 85% to Shs6.51 trillion

The National Social Security Fund reported total revenue of Shs6.51 trillion for the financial year ended June 2026, while assets under management reached Shs32.8 trillion.

The figures were reported as the fund continued to expand its financial operations and digital services. NSSF says it is also targeting more savers, including Ugandans living abroad.

Source: Uganda Radio Network, September 16, 2026.
UGANDA • ECONOMY

Local governments urged to boost revenue and invest in industrialisation

Government officials have urged local governments to strengthen domestic revenue mobilisation and invest in industrialisation as preparations begin for the 2027/2028 budget.

Minister of State for Finance Amos Lugoloobi said revenue-led fiscal consolidation will be among the strategic shifts guiding the next budget. The message was delivered during a regional consultative workshop in Lira.

EDUCATION • UGANDA

Agriculture leads Uganda vocational assessment results

Uganda Vocational and Technical Assessment Board results show strong performance among National Diploma agriculture candidates, with 98.2 percent reported to have attained full competence.

Education officials also highlighted the continuing challenge of attracting more students into agriculture and other technical programmes despite demand for practical skills.
