TOP STORIES • TUESDAY 13 OCTOBER

Fresh verified reports from Uganda and Africa

Uganda: The country marked 64 years of independence on 9 October, with an official message reflecting on sovereignty and economic development.

Business: Daily Monitor reports currency in circulation has risen 12% to Shs10 trillion, citing Bank of Uganda figures.

Transport: Entebbe International Airport is reported to be upgrading its car-parking system with AI technology to help address congestion.

Sports: Uganda’s Sand Cranes are preparing for the first leg of their Beach Soccer AFCON qualifying tie against Burundi on 10 October.

Africa: Reuters reports South Africa has withdrawn an asylum directive after implementation difficulties, while Ethiopia and Eritrea continue to trade accusations amid border tensions.

Sources: Uganda Media Centre, Daily Monitor, EWN, WHO and Reuters. Reports are summarized in original wording and attributed to their publishers.

EDUCATION • UGANDA

UCE examinations begin amid school-fees disputes and rain delays

Reports from the opening of the Uganda Certificate of Education examinations describe disputes over unpaid school fees and rain-related delays in collecting examination papers. UNEB has warned schools not to deny candidates their examinations over unpaid balances.

SPORTS • UGANDA

Uganda Cubs open U-17 AFCON qualifying campaign with 8–0 win

Uganda’s Under-17 team made a strong start to its 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying campaign with an 8–0 victory over Burundi in Kigali.
