TODAY'S EDITION • TUESDAY

The verified stories Uganda and Mbale readers need to know today

This briefing covers the strongest verified developments available on September 15, 2026. Reports are attributed to the named sources. Allegations and disputed claims are labelled as such.

Published September 15, 2026 • Mbale Media Daily
MBALE • HEALTH

UNICEF highlights community health workers' role in Mbale City service delivery

UNICEF Uganda says community health extension workers attached to Namakwekwe Health Centre III are helping strengthen routine services in Mbale City, including child assessments and links to facility care. The programme is being supported by UNICEF and the Mastercard Foundation.

Photo: UNICEF Uganda / Balikuddembe.
Source: UNICEF Uganda, September 15, 2026
EASTERN UGANDA • PUBLIC LAND

Government land inventory rollout reaches Mbale amid anti-grabbing warning

Uganda's state minister for lands Harriet Ntabazi warned officials in eastern Uganda against encroaching on public land as the national inventory programme reached Mbale. The rollout is intended to identify, survey and digitally register government-owned parcels, including land used by schools, hospitals and government offices.

Source: TribeDart, September 13, 2026. The warning and rollout details are attributed to the report.
UGANDA • GOVERNMENT

Government prepares Shs20 billion support for Kampala flood-affected traders

Prime Minister Robinah Nabbanja says government is preparing to distribute Shs20 billion pledged for traders affected by the October 2025 Kampala floods. The Office of the Prime Minister said a verified list identifies 737 traders for consideration, with the list to be displayed for public checking before payments begin.

Source: Uganda Broadcasting Corporation, September 15, 2026
UGANDA • TOORO KINGDOM

Tooro Supreme Council endorses Kijanangoma as King Oyo's successor

Daily Monitor reports that the Tooro Supreme Council has endorsed Edward Rukidi Kijanangoma as successor to the late King Oyo Nyimba. The succession issue has remained sensitive because members of Oyo's family have disputed the process and referred to an alleged will and a claimed son. Those claims remain disputed and should not be treated as established fact without authoritative confirmation.

Sources: Daily Monitor, September 15, 2026; Associated Press, September 12, 2026
UGANDA • ECONOMY

Shilling pressure rises as oil prices climb above $109 a barrel

Daily Monitor reports that the Ugandan shilling weakened past Shs3,900 as international oil prices climbed. Reuters reported that Brent crude rose above $109 on September 15 after supply disruptions affecting Saudi Arabia and Libya, raising concerns about fuel and transport costs if the pressure persists.

Sources: Daily Monitor, September 15, 2026; Reuters, September 15, 2026
AFRICA • SECURITY

South African police investigate killings of seven women near Johannesburg

South African police are investigating the deaths of seven women whose bodies were found over roughly two months in and around Ekurhuleni, east of Johannesburg. Reuters reports that authorities have not determined whether the deaths are connected. President Cyril Ramaphosa has pledged a full investigation.

Source: Reuters, September 15, 2026
HEALTH • AFRICA

UN says Ebola response in eastern Congo is improving, but outbreak remains severe

Reuters reports that daily Ebola cases in eastern Congo have fallen from a mid-August peak, but health officials say it is too early to declare the outbreak past its peak. North Kivu remains the most concerning area because of rising cases, insecurity and population movement.

Source: Reuters, September 15, 2026
TECHNOLOGY • AI

AI access and regulation dominate a new global policy debate

Maria Ressa has called for stronger limits on unchecked artificial intelligence, warning about manipulation, loss of human agency and weak accountability. Separately, the Gates Foundation announced a $1 billion initiative over two years to expand AI applications in health, education, agriculture and less-supported languages. These are policy positions and funding commitments, not evidence that specific AI systems are safe or effective in every setting.

Sources: Reuters, September 15, 2026; Associated Press, September 15, 2026
SPORTS • UGANDA

Cricket Cranes continue Continental Cup campaign in Kigali

Uganda's Cricket Cranes entered the ILT20 Continental Cup with three wins, including victories over Sierra Leone, Botswana and Rwanda, according to Ugandan sports reporting. The campaign continues in Kigali, with results and fixtures subject to change as matches are played.

Sources: Daily Monitor, NBS Sport and ChampionSport reports dated September 10–13, 2026.
COMMUNITY • HUMAN INTEREST

Rainmakers in Uganda face growing pressure as weather becomes less predictable

An Associated Press feature reports that rainmakers in Uganda and neighbouring South Sudan are increasingly blamed when rainfall is too little or too much. The report links the tension to dependence on rain-fed agriculture and growing climate uncertainty, while noting that traditional rainmaking practices continue in some communities.

Source: Associated Press, September 15, 2026
