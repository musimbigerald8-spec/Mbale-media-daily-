Education • National

Uganda schools reopen today for third term

Schools following Uganda's national/public curriculum begin third term today, Monday September 14, 2026. The term is scheduled to run for 82 days and close on December 4.

The Ministry of Education and Sports has maintained the official 2026 school calendar after misinformation circulated about the reopening date. The ministry's published calendar identifies September 14 as the start of Term III.

The reopening applies to pre-primary, primary and secondary schools following the national/public curriculum. Parents and guardians should follow their school's reporting instructions while using the official Ministry calendar as the national reference for the term dates.

Health • National

Government orders mandatory 21-day quarantine for students travelling from DRC

Uganda has ordered a mandatory 21-day quarantine period for students travelling from the Democratic Republic of Congo as health authorities maintain precautionary monitoring.

The reported measure is part of Uganda's precautionary health response to cross-border movement. A 21-day monitoring period gives health teams time to observe affected travellers and respond if symptoms emerge.

Students and families affected by the measure should follow instructions from Uganda's health authorities and should not bypass official screening, monitoring or quarantine arrangements.

Schools may also need to coordinate with health officials where affected learners are due to report for the new academic term. Individual travel and quarantine decisions should be confirmed through the responsible authorities.
