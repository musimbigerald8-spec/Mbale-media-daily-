About Mbale Media Daily

Mbale Media Daily is a digital newsroom serving Mbale, Eastern Uganda, Uganda and audiences beyond the region. Our purpose is to inform, connect and empower communities through accurate, timely and responsible journalism.

Who We Are

We cover local communities, national developments, sports, entertainment and global stories while giving local voices a place in the wider conversation.

Our newsroom is built around clear reporting, responsible storytelling, transparency and the belief that Every Story Matters.

Our Mission

To inform communities, amplify local voices and make reliable news accessible through modern digital media.
