Mbale businesses prepare for World Tourism Day opportunities

September 8, 2026 • Mbale • By Mbale Media Daily

Businesses in Mbale are being encouraged to position themselves for increased visitor activity around World Tourism Day celebrations scheduled for September 26–27.

The celebrations are being viewed as an opportunity for hospitality, transport, culture and local-product businesses to attract visitors and build longer-term tourism markets.

For Mbale, tourism activity can connect visitors to the city, Mount Elgon landscapes, cultural sites, food, accommodation and local enterprises. Businesses that improve customer service and clearly package their products can benefit from increased attention.
