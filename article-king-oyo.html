Tooro succession battle intensifies after King Oyo's death

September 10, 2026 • Mbale Media Daily • By Mbale Media Daily

The Tooro Kingdom is facing a dispute over succession following the death of King Oyo Nyimba Kabamba Iguru Rukidi IV, who died on August 27 at the age of 34 while receiving cancer treatment in the United States.

On Wednesday, the kingdom's royal succession committee announced Prince Edward Rukidi Kijanangoma, a cousin of the late king and a news anchor and editor with Uganda Broadcasting Corporation, as the new monarch.

The announcement was followed by objections from members of King Oyo's family. His sister, Princess Ruth Komuntale, said the late king's will indicated that a son, if lawfully recognized as his biological child, should be heir to the throne.

Queen Best Kemigisa also rejected the selection and called for the succession process to take account of the late king's wishes. The dispute has brought renewed attention to the role of royal customs, the will and the legal process governing succession.

Burial preparations continue

King Oyo's body was returned to Uganda earlier this month. His burial is scheduled for Saturday in the Tooro Kingdom, where large numbers of mourners are expected to gather.

King Oyo became internationally known after taking the throne as a three-year-old in 1995. He later became a prominent cultural figure and served as a United Nations goodwill ambassador for HIV and AIDS awareness.
