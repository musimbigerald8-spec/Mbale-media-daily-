Arsenal face Sunderland test as Gunners chase fourth straight win

September 12, 2026 • Football Desk • By Mbale Media Daily
Archive photo: Arsenal vs Sunderland. Photo licensed under CC BY 2.0 via Wikimedia Commons.
SUNDERLAND vs ARSENAL
Saturday, September 12 • 10:00 PM EAT • Stadium of Light

Arsenal travel to the Stadium of Light on Saturday night looking to maintain their perfect start to the 2026/27 Premier League campaign. The reigning champions have won their opening three league matches and arrive after a 1-0 Champions League victory over Napoli.

Sunderland will provide a difficult test on home ground. The Black Cats have taken four points from their first three league matches and also reached the next round of the EFL Cup after beating Hull City 1-0 in midweek.

Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta has urged his players to stay composed and adaptable. He said the team has learned from last season's late 2-2 draw at Sunderland and is preparing for another demanding atmosphere.

Arsenal are aiming for a fourth consecutive Premier League victory. A win would keep the champions' early title defence on a perfect track, while Sunderland will hope home advantage can produce another strong result against the visitors.

Team news: Arsenal's current injury concerns include William Saliba, while Jurrien Timber and Cristhian Mosquera have been listed as doubts in pre-match reporting. Sunderland have injury concerns of their own, including Habib Diarra and Romaine Mundle.

What to watch: Arsenal's attacking quality and ability to control the match against a Sunderland side that can make the Stadium of Light an uncomfortable venue for visiting teams.
