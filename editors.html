Our Newsroom

Mbale Media Daily is a digital news publication covering Mbale, Eastern Uganda, Uganda, Africa and international developments. Our newsroom aims to publish useful, accurate and clearly attributed reporting while giving readers a way to contact us about errors, updates and story tips.

Our editorial work follows the principles set out in our Editorial Policy. We distinguish reporting from opinion, identify sources where appropriate, and correct material errors when they are brought to our attention.

How we handle stories

  • We seek identifiable and credible sources for important claims.
  • We separate confirmed information from allegations, claims and developing reports.
  • We attribute material obtained from other publishers and official institutions.
  • We update stories when significant new verified information becomes available.
  • We acknowledge and correct material errors rather than silently leaving known inaccuracies online.

Corrections and feedback

If you believe a Mbale Media Daily story contains a factual error, please contact the newsroom with the article title, the specific claim and supporting information. Readers can also send news tips and requests for clarification.
