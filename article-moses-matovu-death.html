Afrigo Band icon Moses Matovu dies after Kampala attack

24 September 2026 • Mbale Media Daily News Desk

Veteran musician Moses Matovu has died after he was attacked in Kampala, police and local media reports say.

Daily Monitor reported that Matovu, 77, was attacked early Thursday in Kibuli Market Zone B and later died at Kibuli Hospital. Police said the circumstances and identity of the assailants were not immediately clear.

Matovu was among the founding members of Afrigo Band, formed in 1975, and served for decades as a leader, saxophonist, vocalist and composer.

Sources: Daily Monitor, September 24, 2026; URN reporting carried by The Independent.
