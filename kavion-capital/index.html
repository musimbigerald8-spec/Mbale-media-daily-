Security by design

Production activation should use server-side authentication, encrypted secrets, role-based access, audit trails, KYC/AML controls and a regulated payment/custody setup.

Role separation

Investor, operations, compliance and administrator permissions are kept separate.

Auditability

Financial events should be immutable and traceable from payment confirmation through allocation and withdrawal.

Controlled money movement

Payment processing should be performed through approved providers rather than browser-only balances.

Compliance path

Before accepting public investment money in Uganda, the operating model must be matched to the applicable CMA and payment-system requirements. The platform can be connected to a licensed partner once that structure is established.
