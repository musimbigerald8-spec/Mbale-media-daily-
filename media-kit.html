About Mbale Media Daily

Mbale Media Daily is a digital news platform focused on timely, relevant and trusted stories from Mbale, Eastern Uganda, Uganda and the wider world.

Coverage: Mbale and Eastern Uganda, national Uganda news, business, sports, entertainment and global developments.
Editorial approach: Responsible storytelling, clear attribution and a focus on giving communities a platform to be heard.
