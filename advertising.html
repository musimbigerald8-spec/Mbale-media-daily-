Advertising at Mbale Media Daily

Mbale Media Daily is building a digital audience around Mbale, Eastern Uganda, Uganda and wider regional news. Advertising helps support the continued production and distribution of our journalism.

Advertising does not buy editorial coverage. Commercial relationships should not determine the factual content of our independent news reporting.

Google AdSense

When the site is approved and connected to Google AdSense, advertising may appear in designated spaces on eligible pages. Google and its advertising partners may select advertisements according to their systems, policies and available inventory.

Advertising cookies and privacy choices are explained in our Privacy Policy.

Direct advertising

Local businesses and organizations may contact the newsroom about banner advertising, sponsored campaigns or other commercial opportunities. Proposed commercial material is subject to editorial, legal and brand-safety review.

Ad placement principles

We aim to use clear, non-deceptive advertising placements that do not imitate navigation buttons, headlines, downloads or editorial content. We will avoid advertising formats that unnecessarily interfere with reading or site navigation.
