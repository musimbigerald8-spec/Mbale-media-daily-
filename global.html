WORLD • MARKETS • ENERGY

Global markets pause after oil retreats from four-month high

September 11, 2026 • World Business

Global bond and stock markets steadied on Friday after a recent selloff, as oil prices retreated from a four-month high. Brent crude fell nearly 2% to around $105.90 after touching $109.97, while the benchmark remained sharply higher for the week.

Investors remain focused on Middle East tensions, energy supply risks and the inflation effect of higher fuel costs. Government bond yields have also risen as markets assess the possibility of tighter monetary policy.
