Five Premier League games finish with late drama across England

September 12, 2026 • Football Desk • By Mbale Media Daily

Five Premier League matches that kicked off at 5:00 PM EAT have finished. Chelsea were held by unbeaten Hull City, Liverpool were frustrated by Fulham, Ipswich won a five-goal thriller at Crystal Palace, Nottingham Forest beat Aston Villa and Bournemouth drew with Brentford.

Chelsea 2-2 Hull City

CHE 2 — 2 HUL

Performance: Chelsea started sharply and Morgan Rogers opened the scoring early, but Hull responded with two Mohamed Belloumi goals. João Pedro rescued a point for the Blues. Chelsea showed attacking quality but defensive errors repeatedly gave Hull opportunities. Hull's organisation and resilience were rewarded with a valuable away point, keeping the promoted side unbeaten after four league matches.

Liverpool 0-0 Fulham

LIV 0 — 0 FUL

Performance: Fulham were the brighter and more threatening side for long periods, while Liverpool struggled with passing and attacking cohesion. Fulham's defensive discipline earned a deserved point at Anfield and gave manager Álvaro Arbeloa his first Premier League point of the season.

Crystal Palace 2-3 Ipswich Town

CRY 2 — 3 IPS

Performance: Ipswich showed strong resilience after Crystal Palace twice fought back. The match changed when Palace defender Axel Disasi was sent off for a high challenge. Ipswich used the extra man effectively, with Leif Davis putting the visitors ahead and Zian Flemming scoring the 90th-minute winner after a Davis header hit the bar. Palace's effort with ten men was spirited, but Ipswich finished stronger.

Aston Villa 1-2 Nottingham Forest

AVL 1 — 2 NFO

Performance: Nottingham Forest produced a disciplined away performance and took the lead through Liam Delap shortly after half-time. Villa pushed for a response, but an injury to Ian Maatsen left them short after their substitutions had been used. Substitute Igor Jesus then struck the winner for Forest late on. Villa are still searching for their first Premier League goal of the season.

Bournemouth 2-2 Brentford

BOU 2 — 2 BRE

Performance: Bournemouth and Brentford traded momentum in an entertaining contest. Kevin Schade scored twice for Brentford, while Justin Kluivert and Marcus Tavernier replied for Bournemouth. The hosts improved after the interval and briefly led before Schade's second goal restored parity.

Still to come today: Tottenham Hotspur vs Everton and Sunderland vs Arsenal were scheduled for later kick-offs and are not included in these completed-match results.
