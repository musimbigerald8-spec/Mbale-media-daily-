Mbale City • Security

Police arrest 17 suspected gang members in Mbale blackspots

Police in Elgon Region say 17 suspects have been arrested during an operation targeting alleged criminal gangs in Mbale City and surrounding areas.

The arrests were reported by Uganda Radio Network on September 17, citing Elgon Region Police spokesperson SP Taitika Rogers. Police say the suspects are believed to be linked to gangs operating in identified city blackspots.

The arrests remain allegations under investigation. Mbale Media Daily will follow official police updates and any court proceedings as they become available.

Mbale • Tourism

Mbale prepares to welcome World Tourism Day visitor

Mbale and the wider Elgon tourism region are preparing for World Tourism Day activities, with local attractions and cultural experiences expected to feature prominently.

Uganda Radio Network reported on September 17 that preparations were underway, highlighting waterfalls, Maleya and cultural experiences in the region.

The development puts renewed attention on tourism opportunities around Mount Elgon and the role of digital media in promoting local destinations and businesses.

Uganda • Inclusion

Uganda marks Deaf Awareness Week with focus on access and empowerment

Uganda's Deaf Awareness Week activities conclude on September 18 with attention on the rights, services and participation of deaf people.

Reporting on the national programme says celebrations are being held in Sembabule, with the week focusing on access to social services and empowerment.

Mbale Media Daily will continue highlighting stories about vulnerable and underserved communities, including access to education, healthcare and public services.
