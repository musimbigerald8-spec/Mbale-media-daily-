Our Editorial Mission

Mbale Media Daily is a digital news publication focused on Mbale, Eastern Uganda, Uganda and stories of wider public interest. Our goal is to provide timely, relevant and useful journalism while treating readers and sources fairly.

Original reporting and sources

We aim to publish original reporting, summaries and analysis. When information comes from public records, official statements, interviews, other publications or other external sources, we seek to identify or credit the source and add editorial context rather than simply reproducing another publisher's work.

We do not intentionally publish copied articles as our own. Third-party photographs, videos, audio and other media remain subject to their respective rights and licenses.

Accuracy and verification

We make reasonable efforts to check names, dates, locations, figures and other material facts before publication. Breaking news may develop after an initial report, so stories can be updated as reliable information becomes available.

Corrections

If you believe a published story contains a material factual error, please contact the newsroom at Mbalemediadailymbale@gmail.com with the article link, the disputed information and the evidence supporting the correction. We review credible correction requests and may update a story when appropriate.

Opinion and commentary

Opinion, commentary or analysis should be distinguishable from straight news reporting. The views expressed in opinion material belong to the author and are not automatically the views of Mbale Media Daily.

Advertising and editorial independence

Advertising or sponsorship should not determine the factual content of our news reporting. Sponsored or promotional material should be identified appropriately when it is published.

Reader feedback

Readers can send news tips, corrections and story ideas through our contact page. We may moderate or decline material that is unlawful, abusive, defamatory, misleading or otherwise unsuitable for publication.
