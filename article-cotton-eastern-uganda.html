Eastern Uganda seeks to revive cotton farming as production declines

September 3, 2026 • Eastern Uganda • By Mbale Media Daily

Stakeholders in Eastern Uganda are pushing to revive cotton production after years of decline in an area where the crop was once a major source of household income.

Cotton was historically known as Eastern Uganda's “white gold”, supporting farmers and businesses across districts including Mbale, Bukedea, Pallisa and Kumi. The crop has lost ground as farmers have shifted toward food and alternative cash crops.

The Uganda Cotton Development Organisation has reported that cotton production in Eastern Uganda declined by about 40 percent over the past decade.

Revival efforts include farmer mobilisation and rebuilding interest in cotton production. Afric Co-operative Society Ltd is among organisations involved in encouraging farmers to return to the crop.

A sustainable revival will depend on reliable markets, quality planting material, good agronomic practices, farmer organisation and returns that make cotton competitive with other crops.
