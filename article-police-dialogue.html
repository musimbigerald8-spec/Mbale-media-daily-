Mbale police officers urged to strengthen open dialogue and discipline

September 7, 2026 • Mbale, Uganda • By Mbale Media Daily

Police leadership in Mbale has encouraged officers to use open and constructive dialogue to raise concerns affecting their welfare and work.

Commissioner of Police Paul Nkore held a Baraza with officers at Mbale Police Barracks on September 7. The engagement gave personnel an opportunity to discuss workplace and welfare concerns and to seek practical solutions.

The Uganda Police Force said officers were encouraged to use appropriate channels when raising concerns. The emphasis on dialogue is intended to improve communication, discipline and working relationships inside the service.

For the wider Mbale community, professional conduct and communication within the police service matter because effective community policing depends on public confidence as well as law enforcement.
