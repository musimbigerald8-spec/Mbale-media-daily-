Manchester United return to Europe's elite

Manchester United return to the UEFA Champions League tonight for the first time since 2023, hosting Azerbaijani champions Sabah at Old Trafford. The fixture gives Michael Carrick's side an early opportunity to make a strong start to their European campaign.

United come into the match after a 2–2 Premier League draw away to Everton. Sabah arrive as Azerbaijani champions and are making their league-phase debut in Europe's top club competition.

The match is scheduled for 10:00 PM East Africa Time. UK listings show an 8:00 PM kick-off.
