National • Tooro Kingdom

Tooro succession dispute deepens after King Oyo's death

The Tooro Kingdom is facing a major succession dispute following the death of King Oyo Nyimba Kabamba Iguru Rukidi IV. The royal clan has announced Prince Edward Rukidi Kijanangoma as the new monarch, while members of King Oyo's family are challenging the decision and pointing to the late king's will.

The issue now involves royal customs, the will and the institutions responsible for determining succession. Kingdom leaders have called for calm and a structured process.
