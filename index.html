About Us

Mbale Media Daily is a Ugandan digital media platform focused on timely, responsible and relevant journalism. We cover Mbale, Eastern Uganda, Uganda, Africa and the wider world.

Our mission is to build a trusted multimedia newsroom where journalists, editors, producers and digital teams work together to serve the public with accurate, useful and engaging stories.
