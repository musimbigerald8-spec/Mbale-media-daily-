About Mbale Media Daily

Mbale Media Daily is a Ugandan digital media platform focused on timely, responsible and relevant journalism. Our newsroom covers Mbale, Eastern Uganda, Uganda, Africa and the wider world.

Our ambition is to grow into a multimedia network where journalists, editors, producers and digital teams work together to publish stories across web, video, audio and social platforms.
