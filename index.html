NATIONAL • TOORO KINGDOM

Tooro succession battle intensifies after King Oyo's death

The Tooro Kingdom is facing a dispute over succession following the death of King Oyo Nyimba Kabamba Iguru Rukidi IV, who died on August 27 at the age of 34 while receiving cancer treatment in the United States.

On Wednesday, the royal succession committee announced Prince Edward Rukidi Kijanangoma, a cousin of the late king and a news anchor and editor with Uganda Broadcasting Corporation, as the new monarch.

The announcement was followed by objections from members of King Oyo's family. His sister, Princess Ruth Komuntale, said the late king's will indicated that a son, if lawfully recognized as his biological child, should be heir to the throne.

Queen Best Kemigisa also rejected the selection and called for the succession process to take account of the late king's wishes. The dispute has brought renewed attention to royal customs, the will and the legal process governing succession.

EASTERN UGANDA • AGRICULTURE

Eastern Uganda moves to revive cotton farming

Cotton was once a major source of income for households across eastern Uganda. Production has declined sharply over the past decade as farmers have increasingly turned to food crops and other cash crops.

Stakeholders are now seeking to rebuild cotton growing, with cooperative organisations working to mobilise farmers and restore production in districts including Mbale, Bukedea, Pallisa and Kumi.
