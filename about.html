About Mbale Media Daily

Mbale Media Daily is a digital news platform focused on Mbale, Eastern Uganda and important stories from across Uganda.

Our aim is to provide detailed, readable reporting that keeps readers on Mbale Media instead of sending them away just to understand a story. We rewrite and contextualize publicly reported information, credit original sources and identify illustrative photographs honestly.

Our coverage includes community affairs, agriculture and coffee, health, education, business, tourism, national news, culture, video and audio.

Editorial principle: Accuracy, attribution, clarity and respect for the people and communities we cover.
