Leadership • Mbale Media Daily

Gerald

Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Gerald is the Chief Executive Officer of Mbale Media Daily, a digital news platform focused on bringing timely, relevant and trusted stories from Mbale, Eastern Uganda, Uganda and the wider world.

His multidisciplinary academic background spans Journalism, Information and Communication Technology (ICT), Coffee Agronomy and Theology.

His leadership is centered on responsible storytelling, digital media growth and giving communities a platform to be heard.
