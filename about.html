Leadership • Mbale Media Daily

Gerald

Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Gerald is the Chief Executive Officer of Mbale Media Daily, a digital news platform focused on bringing timely, relevant and trusted stories from Mbale, Eastern Uganda, Uganda and the wider world.

With a multidisciplinary academic background spanning Journalism, Information and Communication Technology (ICT), Coffee Agronomy and Theology, he brings together communication, digital skills, agricultural knowledge and a strong interest in faith and community life.

His approach to media is centered on telling real stories clearly and responsibly while giving communities a platform to be heard. At Mbale Media Daily, his leadership supports reporting across local affairs, national news, agriculture, business, health, sports, community stories and global developments.

Leadership at Mbale Media Daily

As CEO, Gerald provides direction for the publication's editorial identity and digital growth. The goal is to build a respected media platform that combines local reporting with national, regional and international coverage.

Mbale Media Daily aims to make Eastern Uganda's stories visible while maintaining a professional, responsible and reader-focused approach to journalism.
