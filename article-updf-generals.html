Two retired UPDF generals detained as intelligence probe widens

September 9–10, 2026 • Uganda

Retired Major General Abel Kandiho and retired Brigadier General Charles Bakahumura have been detained as investigations into alleged false intelligence and treachery widen.

Both officers previously headed the Chieftaincy of Military Intelligence, now known as Defence Intelligence and Security. They were among 19 generals retired on August 20, 2026 and were picked up less than three weeks after leaving active service, according to reporting based on military sources.

The investigation remains ongoing and the allegations have not been established in court. Mbale Media Daily will distinguish confirmed developments from claims as the case progresses.

Reporting basis: Daily Monitor and The Independent/URN, September 9–10, 2026. Mbale Media Daily has rewritten the reporting rather than reproducing the original article.
