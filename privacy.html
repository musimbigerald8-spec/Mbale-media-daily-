Privacy Policy

Mbale Media Daily respects the privacy of visitors to our website. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use our website and how it may be used.

Last updated: September 12, 2026.

Information we may receive

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages and access times may be processed by hosting, security, analytics or other technical services in accordance with their own policies.

If you contact the newsroom, the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address and message, may be used to respond to your enquiry, news tip, correction request or advertising enquiry.

The current website subscription form stores the entered email address locally in the visitor's browser. It does not currently send the address to a newsletter provider.

Cookies and advertising

Mbale Media Daily may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, audience measurement and advertising after advertising services are enabled.

When Google AdSense or another Google advertising service is used, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve personalized advertisements based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet, where applicable and permitted. Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

Other advertising partners, if added, may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Where required, Mbale Media Daily will identify applicable advertising vendors and provide the relevant choices and disclosures.

Consent and privacy choices

Where applicable law requires consent for cookies, personalized advertising or similar technologies, the website may display a consent or privacy-choice message. Users may change their choices through the available privacy controls.

Visitors in jurisdictions with specific privacy rights may contact us to ask about their personal information or privacy choices.

Third-party websites and media

Mbale Media Daily may link to external websites or embed media from third-party services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, legal obligations, dispute resolution or legitimate business needs.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for advertising purposes.
