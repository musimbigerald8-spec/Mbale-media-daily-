SC Villa beat Express 2-0 in Kampala derby

September 11, 2026 • Sports Desk • By Mbale Media Daily

SC Villa defeated Express FC 2-0 in the Kampala derby at Muteesa II Stadium, Wankulukuku, on September 9, 2026, continuing a strong start to the Uganda Premier League campaign.

Patrick Jonah Kakande put Villa ahead early, while Denis Otim sealed the result in stoppage time after Express had created opportunities without finding an equaliser.

The victory moved SC Villa to seven points from their opening three league matches. Express, meanwhile, remained on three points after three games.

The match showed the importance of finishing chances in a derby: Express threatened after the interval, but Villa's defensive organisation and late second goal secured all three points.
