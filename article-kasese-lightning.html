Lightning kills two in Kasese during heavy rain

24 September 2026 • Mbale Media Daily News Desk

Two men were killed by lightning in Kasese Municipality during a heavy downpour, police said.

The incident occurred in Nyakatonzi Cell on Tuesday afternoon. Police identified the victims as Jeremiah Baluku and Isaac Kihara.

Police said the two had been operating from separate makeshift shelters but sought refuge together when the rain intensified. Authorities urged residents and institutions to take lightning precautions.

Sources: Daily Monitor, September 23, 2026.
