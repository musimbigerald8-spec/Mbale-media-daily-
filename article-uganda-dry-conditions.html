Dry conditions put pressure on farmers and families across Uganda

September 9–10, 2026 • Uganda

Prolonged dry conditions are affecting water sources, crops and livestock in several parts of Uganda, with communities in areas including the north-east, Bugisu and West Nile facing pressure.

For farmers, dry weather can reduce crop performance and increase pressure on livestock as pasture and water become harder to access. Families may also face higher costs when food production falls.

Uganda's September–December 2026 seasonal climate outlook says many areas are expected to transition from intermittent or isolated rains to more consistent rainfall later in September, with peak rainfall in many eastern areas around late October to November.

For the Mount Elgon region, including Mbale, Sironko, Bulambuli, Manafwa, Bududa, Namisindwa, Kapchorwa, Kween and Bukwo, official guidance expects intermittent rains through mid-to-late September followed by more consistent rainfall, with a peak around mid-October to mid-November.

Farmers can use climate information to plan planting, conserve soil and water, diversify crops and improve post-harvest handling.

Sources: Daily Monitor and Uganda Media Centre seasonal climate outlook. Mbale Media Daily summary.
