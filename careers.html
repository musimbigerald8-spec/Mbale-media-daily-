Careers, Internships & Volunteering

Join Mbale Media Daily and gain practical newsroom experience while contributing to credible journalism and community storytelling across Eastern Uganda.

🎓 Internship Programme

We welcome students and recent graduates who want practical experience in journalism, reporting, photography, video, digital media, social media and newsroom operations.

Interns may receive:

• Practical newsroom experience
• Mentorship and editorial training
• Portfolio-building opportunities
• A certificate after successful completion, subject to programme requirements

🤝 Volunteer Programme

Volunteers can support community reporting, story gathering, photography, media production and other approved newsroom activities.

Our commitment:

Volunteers who successfully complete the agreed programme and meet Mbale Media Daily requirements may be awarded a certificate recognizing their service.

Apply to Join Mbale Media Daily

Complete the form below. We will review your application and contact you using the details you provide.

Certificate policy: A certificate is not automatic simply because an application is submitted. Certificates are awarded after successful completion of the approved internship or volunteer programme and fulfillment of Mbale Media Daily's requirements.
