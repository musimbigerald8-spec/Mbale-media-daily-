Tooro succession dispute deepens as family challenges new king

September 11, 2026 • National Desk

The succession dispute in Uganda's Tooro Kingdom has intensified after a royal clan committee selected Prince Edward Rukidi Kijanangoma as the next monarch, while members of the late King Oyo Nyimba's family challenged the decision.

Kijanangoma, a cousin of the late king and a news anchor and editor at Uganda Broadcasting Corporation, was announced as the committee's choice on September 9. The announcement was followed by objections from Princess Ruth Komuntale and Queen Mother Best Kemigisa.

The family says King Oyo's 2022 will provided for a son, if lawfully recognised as his biological son, to inherit the throne. The existence and eligibility of that son have become central questions in the dispute.

Uganda's Prime Minister of Tooro, Calvin Armstrong Rwomiire Akiiki, has called for calm while the will, traditional customs and applicable legal procedures are considered. The late king is scheduled to be buried at the Karambi royal tombs on Saturday.

Tooro is a culturally influential traditional kingdom. Its monarch does not hold sovereign political power, but the institution remains important to the kingdom's cultural identity.
