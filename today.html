COMMUNITY • INCLUSION

Uganda calls for greater inclusion of deaf people

Uganda's Deaf Awareness Week concludes on September 18 with calls for better access to education, healthcare, employment and public services. The Uganda Media Centre says model secondary schools for deaf learners are operating in Mbale and Wakiso.

Source: Uganda Media Centre, September 16, 2026.
BUSINESS • PENSIONS

NSSF reports Shs32.8 trillion in assets under management

NSSF reported assets of Shs32.8 trillion for the year to June, up 26 percent, while reported revenue reached Shs6.5 trillion. Uganda Business noted that part of the reported revenue increase was not collected as cash.

Sources: Uganda Business, September 18, 2026.
ENERGY • INFRASTRUCTURE

Uganda breaks ground on 320-million-litre fuel storage terminal

A new Kampala Storage Terminal has been commissioned in Mpigi as a strategic storage facility intended to strengthen petroleum supply capacity for domestic and regional markets.

Sources: New Vision and regional reporting, September 17–18, 2026.
SPORTS • CECAFA U-20

Sudan win 3–0 as CECAFA U-20 qualifiers continue

Sudan opened their Group B campaign with a 3–0 victory over Djibouti in Dar es Salaam. Burundi has also reached the semi-final stage according to tournament reporting.

Source: Africa Top Sports, September 18, 2026.
SPORTS • UGANDA CRANES

FUFA confirms ticket prices for Uganda vs Libya

FUFA has confirmed ticket prices for Uganda's AFCON 2027 qualifier against Libya in Hoima on September 29, with tickets available online.

Source: Kawowo Sports, September 18, 2026.
ENTERTAINMENT • MBALE

Joshua Baraka's Mbale music journey puts Eastern Uganda in the spotlight

Ugandan music star Joshua Baraka took his B'rakaland East Africa tour to Mbale as part of his Keep Walking campaign, connecting with fans in Eastern Uganda.

Source: Pulse Uganda, September 11, 2026.
