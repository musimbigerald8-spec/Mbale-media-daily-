Uganda withdraws from Invictus Games, military chief cites King Charles

September 11, 2026 • National Desk • By Mbale Media Daily

Uganda has announced that it will withdraw from the Invictus Games, an international sporting event for wounded, injured and sick service personnel and veterans.

Gen Muhoozi Kainerugaba, Uganda's military chief, announced the decision on September 9 and said Uganda would not participate in an event without the approval of King Charles III.

Uganda had earlier announced plans to participate in the Games, making the withdrawal a significant change in the country's position. The decision has drawn attention because the Invictus Games are closely associated with Prince Harry and the wider British royal family.

The announcement is political and diplomatic in its implications as well as sporting. Mbale Media Daily will continue to follow any response from the organisers and Ugandan authorities.
