Houthi advance along Yemen's Red Sea coast raises shipping concerns

September 12, 2026 • Global Desk

Nearly 50,000 civilians have fled parts of western Yemen after a rapid advance by Houthi forces along the Red Sea coast, reports said on September 12, citing the United Nations' International Organization for Migration.

The advance has included strategic coastal areas and has intensified concern about the security of shipping near the Bab al-Mandab strait, a major maritime route linking the Red Sea with the Gulf of Aden. Aid access has also been affected by damaged infrastructure and blocked roads.

The humanitarian situation remains severe, with civilians facing displacement, food insecurity and limited access to healthcare. The wider regional impact could include higher shipping, fuel and insurance costs if commercial traffic is disrupted.

Source and verification: This report is based on Reuters and The Guardian reporting published September 12, 2026, with humanitarian details attributed to the UN migration agency. Claims by the parties to the conflict are not independently verified.
