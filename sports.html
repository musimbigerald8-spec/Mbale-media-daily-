CRICKET • UGANDA

Cricket Cranes remain among the teams to watch in Kigali

September 18, 2026 • Africa Continental Cup

Uganda's Cricket Cranes entered the Africa Continental Cup with the ambition of defending their continental title. Their early campaign has produced convincing performances, including a 114-run victory over Sierra Leone, a five-wicket win over Botswana and a 64-run victory over Rwanda.

The results have been built around aggressive batting and pressure through the bowling innings. Uganda's ability to score quickly while still taking regular wickets has helped create large winning margins and a strong net run rate.

The tournament continues this week with Uganda scheduled to meet Sierra Leone on September 15, Botswana on September 16, Rwanda on September 17 and Kenya on September 18. Those matches will test whether the Cranes can maintain their early momentum through the decisive phase of the competition.

Sources: published Africa Continental Cup results and schedules. Uganda beat Rwanda by 64 runs on September 11; the next listed Uganda fixture is Sierra Leone on September 15.
FOOTBALL • CAF CHAMPIONS LEAGUE

Vipers hold Nouadhibou to 0–0 away from home

September 13, 2026 • Mauritania

Vipers SC drew 0–0 with FC Nouadhibou in the CAF Champions League return meeting in Mauritania. The result followed the 1–1 draw between the sides at St Mary's Stadium, Kitende in the first leg.

The Ugandan champions can take encouragement from keeping a clean sheet away from home. At the same time, the absence of a winning goal means the team needed to rely on its overall competition position rather than a decisive attacking result in the return match.

The fixture was a demanding test of defensive organisation and concentration. Vipers will now turn their attention to domestic competition and the next stage of their continental programme.

Source basis: Vipers SC official fixture record and published match data.
PREMIER LEAGUE • ARSENAL

Arsenal survive Sunderland challenge to keep perfect start

September 13, 2026 • Sunderland 0–2 Arsenal

Arsenal maintained their perfect Premier League start with a 2–0 victory at Sunderland. The Gunners had entered the match with three wins from three and finished the weekend with a fourth straight league victory.

Sunderland made Arsenal work for the result. The hosts created pressure and had an important penalty opportunity, but Arsenal goalkeeper David Raya produced a crucial save. Arsenal then found the breakthrough through Bruno Guimarães before Bukayo Saka converted a late penalty.

The result highlights Arsenal's ability to win difficult away matches even when the performance is not completely comfortable. The defending champions now carry early-season momentum into the next round of fixtures.

Source: Anadolu Agency match report published September 13, 2026.
UGANDA PREMIER LEAGUE

SC Villa's strong start adds pressure to early-season race

September 9–11, 2026 • Uganda

SC Villa's early results have put the Jogoos among the sides making a positive start to the Uganda Premier League season. Villa defeated Express FC 2–0 on September 9, while Mbarara City and NEC FC shared a goalless draw two days later.

Early-season results are still developing, but the opening fixtures are already showing the importance of consistency. Teams that collect points before the calendar becomes congested can build an advantage in the race for continental places and the league title.

Mbale Media Daily will continue following the Uganda Premier League with special attention to clubs from Eastern Uganda and the wider regional football community.

Source basis: published Uganda Premier League results.
UGANDA CRANES • AFCON PREPARATION

New-look Uganda Cranes squad begins the next phase of AFCON preparation

September 2026 • National Team

Uganda Cranes head coach Paul Put has named a 28-man squad as the national team prepares for the next stage of its Africa Cup of Nations qualification campaign. The squad contains a mixture of established players and newer faces.

The selection comes as Uganda continues building towards a competitive international calendar. Friendlies and qualifiers give the coaching staff an opportunity to assess combinations, develop depth and identify players capable of performing under pressure.

For Ugandan supporters, the key question will be whether the new-look squad can translate preparation into consistent performances when competitive matches arrive.

Source basis: Daily Monitor reporting on Paul Put's September 2026 squad announcement.
