ATHLETICS

Uganda tightens rules for road races

September 8, 2026 • Athletics

Uganda Athletics has introduced tighter licensing requirements for marathons, road races, cross-country, mountain and trail events. Organisers are expected to meet federation requirements, use qualified technical officials and provide certified routes where required.

The changes are intended to improve safety, organisation and standards across road-running events.

Uganda wins CAF African Schools Football CECAFA boys' qualifier

September 8, 2026 • Kigali

Uganda's boys won the CAF African Schools Football Championship CECAFA qualifier in Kigali after Kennedy Okwi scored the decisive goal against Rwanda in the final.

The result gives Uganda another strong moment in youth football and highlights the importance of school competitions in developing the next generation of players.
