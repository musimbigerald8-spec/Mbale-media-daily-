TODAY'S SPORTS • PREMIER LEAGUE RESULTS

Five Premier League games finish with late drama

September 12, 2026 • Updated after the 5:00 PM EAT kick-offs

Five Premier League matches have finished. Chelsea drew 2-2 with Hull City, Liverpool drew 0-0 with Fulham, Crystal Palace lost 2-3 to Ipswich Town, Aston Villa lost 1-2 to Nottingham Forest and Bournemouth drew 2-2 with Brentford.

Chelsea 2-2 Hull City

CHE 2 — 2 HUL

Performance: Chelsea started strongly through Morgan Rogers but defensive errors allowed Mohamed Belloumi to score twice for Hull. João Pedro equalised late. Chelsea's attack remained dangerous, but Hull showed excellent resilience and remain unbeaten after four league matches.

Venue image: Stamford Bridge, Wikimedia Commons.

Liverpool 0-0 Fulham

LIV 0 — 0 FUL

Performance: Fulham were the brighter and more threatening side, while Liverpool struggled with passing and attacking cohesion. Fulham's defensive discipline earned a valuable point at Anfield.

Venue image: Anfield, Wikimedia Commons.

Crystal Palace 2-3 Ipswich Town

CRY 2 — 3 IPS

Performance: Ipswich stayed composed after Palace twice fought back. Palace defender Axel Disasi was sent off for a high challenge, and Ipswich used the extra man effectively. Leif Davis put Ipswich ahead before Zian Flemming struck the 90th-minute winner.

Venue image: Selhurst Park, Wikimedia Commons.

Aston Villa 1-2 Nottingham Forest

AVL 1 — 2 NFO

Performance: Forest were disciplined away from home. Liam Delap opened the scoring and substitute Igor Jesus later produced the winner. Villa were affected by Ian Maatsen's injury after their substitutions had been used and remain without a Premier League win after four matches.

Venue image: Villa Park, Wikimedia Commons.

Bournemouth 2-2 Brentford

BOU 2 — 2 BRE

Performance: Brentford's Kevin Schade scored twice, while Justin Kluivert and Marcus Tavernier replied for Bournemouth. The match swung from side to side, with Bournemouth briefly taking the lead before Schade restored parity.

Venue image: Vitality Stadium, Wikimedia Commons.
