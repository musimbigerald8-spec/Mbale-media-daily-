Uganda Cranes draw 1–1 with Tunisia in AFCON 2027 qualifier

Uganda came from behind to draw 1–1 with Tunisia in Radès on September 24. Adem Arous put Tunisia ahead in the 12th minute before Rogers Mato equalised for Uganda in the 68th minute.

September 24, 2026 • AFCON 2027 Qualifiers, Group H

UGANDA CRANES • AFCON 2027

Rogers Mato rescues Uganda against Tunisia

Uganda conceded early but responded in the second half, with Rogers Mato scoring in the 68th minute after an assist from Nathan Asiimwe. The Cranes left Radès with a 1–1 draw in their opening Group H qualifier.

Uganda are already guaranteed a place at the 2027 AFCON finals as co-hosts with Kenya and Tanzania. The qualifying matches are therefore being used to test and develop the squad ahead of the tournament.
