UNEB releases 2026 national examination timetable

24 September 2026 • Mbale Media Daily News Desk

The Uganda National Examinations Board has released the 2026 timetable for PLE, UCE and UACE examinations.

Uganda Media Centre reported 1,627,885 registered candidates: 920,796 for PLE, 503,214 for UCE and 203,875 for UACE.

UCE briefing is October 8, with examinations through November 3. PLE briefing is October 30, with written examinations ending November 4. UACE briefings are November 6–7, with examinations ending December 5.

UNEB also warned against examination malpractice and fraud involving alleged leaked papers.

Sources: Uganda Media Centre, September 23, 2026; Daily Monitor, September 24, 2026.
