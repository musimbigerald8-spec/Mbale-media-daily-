Coffee seedlings dry up in Katakwi as farmers question planting timing

September 7, 2026 • Katakwi, Teso

Farmers in Katakwi are reporting losses after thousands of government-distributed coffee seedlings dried up during a prolonged dry spell.

The affected programme forms part of a wider push to expand commercial agriculture in Teso. Farmers say the timing of distribution during dry conditions made it difficult to keep seedlings alive and are calling for better planning, water access and farmer training.

The report says the government had launched a campaign to distribute more than 80 million high-value coffee seedlings across the Teso sub-region, with the aim of moving more farmers toward commercial crops.

The episode highlights the importance of matching seedling distribution with seasonal conditions and ensuring farmers have practical support to establish new crops successfully.

Source: Mbale Media Daily summary of reporting on the Katakwi coffee seedling losses, September 7, 2026.
