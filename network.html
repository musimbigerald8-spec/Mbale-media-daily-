The Mbale Media Network

We are building Mbale Media Daily into a home-grown multimedia newsroom serving Mbale, Eastern Uganda and audiences beyond — connecting journalism, video, audio, sport, entertainment, production and digital storytelling.

Our ecosystem

One brand. Multiple media platforms.

The network is designed so one strong story can move across the right platforms without losing editorial ownership. This follows the integrated-media principle we studied from established Ugandan multimedia groups, while keeping Mbale Media's own identity, voice and editorial direction.

The newsroom engine

How one story moves through Mbale Media

01 · REPORTJournalist gathers facts, interviews and media.
02 · SUBMITStory enters the newsroom workflow for review.
03 · EDITEditor checks accuracy, structure, headline and presentation.
04 · PUBLISHApproved story goes live on Mbale Media Daily.
05 · DISTRIBUTENews can become video, audio and social content.

Built for a growing Ugandan newsroom

Admin remains the highest-level control. Editors can publish when the administrator is away, while journalists submit stories for editorial review.
