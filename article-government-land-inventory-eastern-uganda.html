Government land inventory drive reaches Eastern Uganda

September 11, 2026 • Mbale Media Daily Eastern Uganda Desk

Leaders in Bugisu, Teso and Bukedi have been urged to work with the Ministry of Lands, Housing and Urban Development and the Uganda Land Commission to curb land grabbing, corruption and encroachment on government land.

The call was made by State Minister for Lands Harriet Ntabazi during a regional stakeholders' workshop at Mbale Resort Hotel on the development of a comprehensive government land inventory.

The national programme, launched in July, is intended to identify, survey and digitally register government-owned land, including schools, hospitals, roads, security installations and administrative offices. The records are expected to feed into the National Land Information System.

For Eastern Uganda, the exercise is significant because accurate records can help public institutions defend their land and improve planning. Leaders were encouraged to support the inventory process and report attempts to take or encroach on public land.
