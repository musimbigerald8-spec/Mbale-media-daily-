MBALE & EASTERN UGANDA

Government warns Elgon and other high-risk areas ahead of above-average rains

Uganda's Office of the Prime Minister says the September–December 2026 rainy season may bring heavier-than-average rainfall, flash floods, landslides and disease outbreaks. Authorities are urging residents in high-risk areas, including the Elgon sub-region, to move to safer ground and follow early-warning messages.

Sources: Daily Monitor, September 17, 2026; Uganda Broadcasting Corporation, September 17, 2026

MBALE / TOURISM

Mbale prepares for World Tourism Day spotlight

Mbale is preparing to host Uganda's national World Tourism Day celebrations on September 27, with officials highlighting Mount Elgon, Sipi Falls, cultural attractions and local cuisine as part of Eastern Uganda's tourism offer.

Sources: Uganda Radio Network, September 17, 2026; Uganda Ministry of Tourism, September 4, 2026

UGANDA NATIONAL

Relief and livestock-restocking programmes continue

The Office of the Prime Minister says food relief distribution in Karamoja has been completed and cattle restocking is progressing in Acholi, Lango and Teso. The update was issued alongside the warning on the coming rains.

Source: Uganda Broadcasting Corporation, September 17, 2026
