MBALE • EASTERN UGANDA • HEALTH

484 community health extension workers pass out in Mbale and Bukedea

Uganda's Ministry of Health says 484 Community Health Extension Workers have completed training in Mbale and Bukedea. The ministry said the Eastern Uganda field mission focused on primary-care delivery, hard-to-reach communities and service readiness at facilities including Namatala Health Centre IV.

Source: Uganda Ministry of Health, September 8, 2026. Read source
UGANDA • NATIONAL

Uganda reverses Invictus Games withdrawal after clarification from King Charles

Uganda has reversed its decision to withdraw from the 2027 Invictus Games. The Guardian reported on September 14 that the change followed assurances from King Charles III's principal private secretary that the King supported Uganda's participation. The Games are scheduled for Birmingham in 2027.

Source: The Guardian, September 14, 2026. Read source
UGANDA • JUSTICE

High Court begins hearing 2026 election petitions

Uganda's High Court is scheduled to begin reviewing 118 petitions arising from the January 2026 elections, including 107 parliamentary and 11 local-election petitions. A petition is a legal challenge, not proof that a result was invalid; each case must be decided on evidence and law.

Source: Uganda Judiciary information reported September 14, 2026 by regional media. The cases are scheduled through November 13, 2026.
TOORO • CULTURE

Tooro succession remains disputed after King Oyo's burial

King Oyo Nyimba Kabamba Iguru Rukidi IV was buried on September 12 at the Karambi Royal Tombs. Associated Press reported that elders named his cousin Edward Rukidi Kijanangoma as successor, while members of Oyo's family have disputed the process and referred to an alleged will and claimed son. Those claims remain contested and should not be treated as settled fact.

Sources: Associated Press and Reuters, September 10–13, 2026. Read AP report
AFRICA • DIPLOMACY

Nigeria raises concern over renewed anti-migrant violence in South Africa

Nigeria has raised concern about renewed violence affecting foreign nationals in South Africa. The issue matters beyond the immediate incidents because both countries are major African economies and important diplomatic and commercial partners. Specific allegations should be verified case by case.

Source: Regional reporting reviewed by Mbale Media Daily's Africa desk, September 2026.
BUSINESS • ECONOMY

Uganda trade roadshow puts value addition and regional investment in focus

More than 150 business leaders from 10 countries are reported to be attending a Uganda Trade and Investment Roadshow in Kampala. The programme is highlighting agro-processing, minerals, pharmaceuticals, ICT and cross-border trade, with a focus on moving beyond raw-material exports.

Source: Independent Uganda report summarized September 14, 2026.
TECHNOLOGY • SCIENCE

EU prepares proposal on social media and AI chatbot access for under-15s

Reuters reported on September 14 that the European Union is preparing an EU Kids Act proposal covering social media, video-sharing platforms, AI chatbots and online games. The proposal is not law yet and would still require approval by EU member states and the European Parliament.

Source: Reuters, September 14, 2026. Read source
GLOBAL • MARKETS

AI-linked shares fall as industry leaders call for slower development

Reuters reported that technology stocks fell on September 14 after warnings from AI leaders about safety and the pace of development. Market moves are not evidence that the AI sector has ended; analysts cited in the report offered differing views on whether the decline reflected a temporary correction or a deeper change in investment expectations.

Source: Reuters, September 14, 2026. Read source
SPORTS

Uganda's Cricket Cranes carry early momentum in Kigali

Uganda's Cricket Cranes began their ILT20 Continental Cup campaign strongly in Kigali, with wins reported against Sierra Leone, Botswana and Rwanda. The tournament continues, so the final outcome remains open.
