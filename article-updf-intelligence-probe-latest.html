Two retired UPDF generals detained as intelligence probe widens

September 11, 2026 • National Desk • By Mbale Media Daily

Two retired senior officers of the Uganda People's Defence Forces have reportedly been detained as an investigation into alleged false intelligence and treachery widens.

The officers identified in current reporting are retired Major General Abel Kandiho and retired Brigadier General Charles Bakahumura. Both previously held senior roles in military intelligence.

Their detention follows the retirement of a group of senior generals in August. Reporting indicates that investigators are examining allegations connected to intelligence activities, but the case remains under investigation.

Mbale Media Daily will continue to separate verified developments from allegations as authorities provide more information. Detention or investigation does not by itself establish guilt.

Reporting basis: Daily Monitor and Uganda Radio Network reporting published September 9–10, 2026. Mbale Media Daily has independently rewritten and summarized the reports.
