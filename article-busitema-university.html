Busitema University focuses on results, accountability and impact

September 2026 • Busitema, Eastern Uganda

Busitema University continues to emphasize institutional results, accountability and practical impact as it grows its academic and research work.

Recent university activities have included management discussions focused on institutional performance as well as research work connected to agriculture, irrigation and water management.

The university's location in Eastern Uganda gives its research and training an important regional dimension, particularly in agriculture, engineering and other fields connected to development.
